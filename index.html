    🏛️ Municipality Tycoon

    You are the mayor of Clifton. Every other week the council meets in chambers: you work through an agenda, hear a development proposal, listen to the public, draft conditions, and put it to a vote. On the off weeks you inspect sites, meet residents, and take private meetings.

    Approved projects move through Permitting, Site Prep, Construction, Commissioning and Operation. Expect wells drilled without permits, contaminated water, wasted water, noise, but also new roads, parks and donations. How you respond decides your term.

    Your goal: keep the job and grow Clifton to the population target by the end of your term. You lose if Public Trust collapses, the environment fails, or a corruption scandal lands you in court. Click or press Space/Enter to advance dialogue.
