    🏛️ Municipality Tycoon

    You are the mayor of Clifton. Every week the council meets in chambers: you read city business, hear one development proposal, listen to the public, draft conditions, and put it to a vote.

    Approved projects move through Permitting, Site Prep, Construction, Commissioning and Operation. Expect wells drilled without permits, contaminated water, wasted water, noise, but also new roads, parks and donations. How you respond decides your term.

    You lose if Public Trust collapses, the environment fails, or a corruption scandal lands you in court. Click or press Space/Enter to advance dialogue.
